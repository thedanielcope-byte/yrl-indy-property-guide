Meet the Your Realty Link Team

Your Realty Link is a local boutique brokerage with 20+ experienced agents listing and selling homes on every side of Central Indiana. Our agents are seasoned and tech-savvy, and they work hard to find buyers the perfect home — while our sellers love our competitive commission structure and the extensive marketing plans behind every listing.

We're experts in this market. From Carmel and Zionsville in the north to Greenwood and Bargersville in the south, Plainfield and Avon in the west to McCordsville and New Palestine in the east, our team knows the neighborhoods, school districts, and communities that make Central Indiana home. Our technology lets our agents work from anywhere — but we're proudly a brick-and-mortar office, right across from Long's Bakery in Southport at 2302 E Southport Rd.

Whether you're buying your first home, selling a longtime family property, or building an investment portfolio, there's a Your Realty Link agent ready to help. Meet our agents or schedule a free consultation — and find out why Your Realty Link will be your real estate office for life.

Our Brokerage Story

Your Realty Link was built on a simple idea: real estate should feel less like a transaction and more like a partnership. We're not a corporate franchise with a call center. We're a locally owned, brokerage based in Indianapolis at 2302 E Southport Rd — and we're proud of it.

The brokerage serves the full Indianapolis metro and beyond, covering communities from Zionsville and Carmel in the north to Greenwood and Bargersville in the south, from Plainfield and Avon in the west to McCordsville and New Palestine in the east. If it's Central Indiana, Your Realty Link knows it.

Our agents are agents with access to the full MLS, which means clients get comprehensive market data, real-time listing information, and the kind of negotiating leverage that only comes with being connected to the market at the highest level.

Buyers: From first-time buyers navigating pre-approval to seasoned buyers looking for a move-up home or investment property, YRL represents buyers at every stage. We provide full buyer representation — at no cost to you in most transactions — and guide you through search, offer, inspection, and closing.

Sellers: Our seller services include a free comparative market analysis (CMA), professional listing preparation guidance, full MIBOR MLS exposure, and a marketing plan designed to attract qualified buyers. The YRL team works to get your home sold on your timeline, at a price that works for you.

Investors: Indianapolis is consistently ranked among the top real estate investment markets in the country, and Your Realty Link has the market knowledge to help investors find the right properties — whether that's a single-family rental, a fix-and-flip, or a multi-family asset.

Agents: If you're a licensed Indiana real estate agent looking for a better brokerage home, Your Realty Link offers competitive commission splits, broker support, and a team culture that actually invests in your success. Learn more about joining YRL.
