8 Anderson neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

Anderson sits in Madison County, and like most Central Indiana towns it’s really a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range, age of homes and amenities.

This guide covers the 8 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Anderson that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Country Club Heights

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Country Club Heights is a small incorporated town community in Anderson with homes typically in the $150s to $400s. Schools: Anderson Community School Corporation.

  • Incorporated town (1950) of about 100 residents, surrounded by Anderson and Woodlawn Heights
  • Home to the Anderson Country Club, formed in 1902
  • Mostly 1960s ranches on roughly 1/3- to 1/2-acre lots, plus older homes on the golf course
  • Anderson mailing addresses; Anderson Community Schools; no HOA

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Anderson

What are the best neighborhoods in Anderson, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Anderson’s neighborhoods range from Woodlawn Heights ($120s to $250s) to Deer Creek ($200s to $400s); for waterfront living, Lakes of Killbuck. We group all 8 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Anderson?

Of the 8 neighborhoods in this guide, Woodlawn Heights has the lowest typical price range, about $120s to $250s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

What school district serves Anderson neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Anderson Community Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Anderson neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Anderson, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
