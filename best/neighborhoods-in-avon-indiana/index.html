Best Neighborhoods in Avon, Indiana

13 Avon neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hendricks County 🏡 13 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $1M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Avon’s named neighborhoods run from Westover Woods (typically $200s to $300s) to Parks at Prestwick ($400s to $1M+). For new construction, look at Whispering Pines and Manors at Avon; for a master-planned community, Bridgewater; for golf-course living, Parks at Prestwick; and for less upkeep, Westover Woods. Most are served by Avon Community School Corporation.

Avon is a fast-growing, family-first Hendricks County community known for strong schools, new shopping, and excellent value on the west side. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via US-36 (Rockville Road) and I-74, and homes across Avon typically range from the $250s to the $450s.

This guide covers the 13 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Avon that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Avon

What are the best neighborhoods in Avon, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Avon’s neighborhoods range from Westover Woods ($200s to $300s) to Parks at Prestwick ($400s to $1M+); for new construction, look at Whispering Pines and Manors at Avon; for golf-course living, Parks at Prestwick. We group all 13 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Avon?

Of the 13 neighborhoods in this guide, Westover Woods has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $300s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Avon neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Whispering Pines and Manors at Avon are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Avon?

Yes. Parks at Prestwick is among the Avon neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Avon neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Avon Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Avon neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Avon, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
