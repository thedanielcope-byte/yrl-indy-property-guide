Best Neighborhoods in Bargersville, Indiana

10 Bargersville neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

Quick Answer

Bargersville’s named neighborhoods run from Meadowbrook Acres (typically $100s to $200s) to Aberdeen ($500s to $1M+). For new construction, look at Bellingham, Morningside and Walnut Commons. Most are served by Center Grove Community Schools.

Bargersville sits in Johnson County, and like most Central Indiana towns it’s really a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range, age of homes and amenities.

This guide covers the 10 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Bargersville that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Bargersville

What are the best neighborhoods in Bargersville, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Bargersville’s neighborhoods range from Meadowbrook Acres ($100s to $200s) to Aberdeen ($500s to $1M+); for new construction, look at Bellingham, Morningside and Walnut Commons. We group all 10 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Bargersville?

Of the 10 neighborhoods in this guide, Meadowbrook Acres has the lowest typical price range, about $100s to $200s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Bargersville neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Bellingham, Morningside, Walnut Commons, Sawyer Walk and Highland Knoll are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Bargersville?

Yes. Morningside, Shadowood and Aberdeen are among the Bargersville neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Bargersville neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Center Grove Community Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Bargersville neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Bargersville, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
