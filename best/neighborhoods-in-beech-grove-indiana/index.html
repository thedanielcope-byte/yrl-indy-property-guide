6 Beech Grove neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

Beech Grove sits in Marion County, and like most Central Indiana towns it’s really a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range, age of homes and amenities.

This guide covers the 6 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Beech Grove that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Beech Grove

What are the best neighborhoods in Beech Grove, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Beech Grove’s neighborhoods range from Southeastwood (low-$100s to $200s) to Carrington Park (mid-$100s to high-$200s). We group all 6 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Beech Grove?

Of the 6 neighborhoods in this guide, Southeastwood has the lowest typical price range, about low-$100s to $200s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

What school district serves Beech Grove neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Beech Grove City Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
