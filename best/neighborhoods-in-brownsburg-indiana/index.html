Best Neighborhoods in Brownsburg, Indiana

14 Brownsburg neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hendricks County 🏡 14 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $500s

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Brownsburg’s named neighborhoods run from Country Walk (typically $200s to $380s) to Highland Springs ($350s to $500s). For a master-planned community, Wynne Farms and Eagle Crossing, and for the water, Cedar Run Lake. Most are served by Brownsburg Community School Corporation.

Brownsburg is a welcoming west-side town with racing heritage, top-rated schools, and a growing downtown that keeps a genuine small-town feel. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-74, and homes across Brownsburg typically range from the $250s to the $450s.

This guide covers the 14 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Brownsburg that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Brownsburg

What are the best neighborhoods in Brownsburg, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Brownsburg’s neighborhoods range from Country Walk ($200s to $380s) to Highland Springs ($350s to $500s); for waterfront living, Cedar Run Lake. We group all 14 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Brownsburg?

Of the 14 neighborhoods in this guide, Country Walk has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $380s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

What school district serves Brownsburg neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Brownsburg Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
