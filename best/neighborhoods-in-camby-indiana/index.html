Best Neighborhoods in Camby, Indiana

7 Camby neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Marion County 🏡 7 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – high-$380s

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Camby’s named neighborhoods run from Heartland Crossing (typically $200s to $400s) to Oberlin (high $290s to high $380s). For new construction, look at The Village, Parks at Decatur and Oberlin, and for a master-planned community, Heartland Crossing. Most are served by MSD of Decatur Township.

Camby sits in Marion County, and like most Central Indiana towns it’s really a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range, age of homes and amenities.

This guide covers the 7 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Camby that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Established Family Neighborhoods

Settled subdivisions with mature trees, neighborhood amenities and a track record.

Trotters Landing

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Trotters Landing is an established subdivision in Camby with homes typically in the high $200s to high $300s. Schools: MSD of Decatur Township.

  • Built out by D.R. Horton at Camby Road and Trotter Road
  • Open ranch and two-story layouts
  • Established resale neighborhood

New-Construction Communities

Communities where builders are, or recently were, still building, so you can buy new or nearly new.

Glenwood

Best for: buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

Glenwood is a new-construction community in Camby with homes typically in the high $200s to high $300s. Schools: MSD of Decatur Township.

  • Lennar single-family community with 'Everything's Included' packages
  • Convenient to I-465 and the airport corridor
  • Modern open floor plans

See Glenwood homes and details →

Noble Heights

Best for: buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

Noble Heights is a new-construction community in Camby with homes typically in the high $200s to low $400s. Schools: MSD of Decatur Township.

  • Lennar master-planned community including single-level plans
  • Modern open floor plans with included features
  • Growing southwest-side location near Camby amenities

See Noble Heights homes and details →

Village at Heartland Crossing

Best for: buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

Village at Heartland Crossing is a new-construction community in Camby with homes typically in the $300s to $400s. Schools: Mooresville / MSD of Decatur Township (varies by lot).

  • D.R. Horton ranch and two-story plans within Heartland Crossing
  • Shares master-community pools, parks, and tennis
  • Minutes from dining, shopping, and the airport

See The Village homes and details →

Parks at Decatur

Best for: buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

Parks at Decatur is a new-construction community in Camby with homes typically in the high $290s to low $400s. Schools: MSD of Decatur Township.

  • D.R. Horton homes with smart-home technology
  • Walking trails, fishing pond, playground, and splash pad
  • Feeds Decatur Central schools

See Parks at Decatur homes and details →

Oberlin at Camby Village

Best for: buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

Oberlin at Camby Village is a new-construction community in Camby with homes typically in the high $290s to high $380s. Schools: MSD of Decatur Township.

  • Roughly 184 Taylor Morrison homes with flex rooms, studies, and lofts
  • Meandering trails and a community playground
  • Central to Camby's shopping, dining, and airport access

See Oberlin homes and details →

Master-Planned Communities

Larger planned communities with shared amenities designed in from the start.

Heartland Crossing

Best for: buyers who want built-in amenities.

Heartland Crossing is a master-planned community in Camby with homes typically in the $200s to $400s. Schools: Mooresville, Plainfield, or MSD of Decatur Township (varies by section).

  • Largest community in Decatur Township, wrapped around Heartland Crossings Golf Links
  • Multiple pools, parks, tennis courts, and an HOA clubhouse
  • New-construction sections by D.R. Horton and Ryan Homes; quick I-465 and airport access

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Camby

What are the best neighborhoods in Camby, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Camby’s neighborhoods range from Heartland Crossing ($200s to $400s) to Oberlin (high $290s to high $380s); for new construction, look at The Village, Parks at Decatur and Oberlin. We group all 7 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Camby?

Of the 7 neighborhoods in this guide, Heartland Crossing has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $400s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Camby neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, The Village, Parks at Decatur, Oberlin, Glenwood and Noble Heights are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

What school district serves Camby neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by MSD of Decatur Township. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Camby neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Camby, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
