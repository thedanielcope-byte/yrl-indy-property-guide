Best Neighborhoods in Carmel, Indiana

17 Carmel neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hamilton County 🏡 17 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $3M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Carmel’s named neighborhoods run from Village of Mount Carmel (typically $200s to $500s) to Crooked Stick Estates ($800s to $3M+). For new construction, look at The Sanctuary at 116th; for a master-planned community, Village of WestClay and Jackson's Grant; for golf-course living, Brookshire and Plum Creek; and for the water, Woodland Springs. Most are served by Carmel Clay Schools.

Carmel is an upscale, amenity-rich suburb famous for its roundabouts, the Palladium concert hall, the Monon Trail, and a genuinely walkable downtown. It’s roughly 20 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via US-31/Meridian Street and Keystone Parkway, and homes across Carmel typically range from the mid-$400s to well over $1 million.

This guide covers the 17 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Carmel that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Luxury & Estate Neighborhoods

The top of the local market: custom and estate homes, larger lots, club amenities.

The Sanctuary at 116th Street

Best for: luxury buyers and buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

The Sanctuary at 116th Street is a newer luxury community in Carmel with homes typically in the $500s to $900s. Schools: Carmel Clay Schools.

  • Newer construction
  • Mix of single-family, townhomes and condos
  • Low-maintenance living options

See The Sanctuary at 116th homes and details →

Crooked Stick Estates

Best for: luxury buyers and golfers.

Crooked Stick Estates is a luxury golf community in Carmel with homes typically in the $800s to $3M+. Schools: Carmel Clay Schools.

  • Custom homes around the Pete Dye-designed Crooked Stick Golf Club (host of the 1991 PGA Championship)
  • Lots typically about 3/4 acre to 2+ acres, many on the course or the lake
  • Mostly built late 1960s to about 1990, plus some newer rebuilds
  • Off Ditch Road between 106th and 116th Streets in west Carmel

See Crooked Stick Estates homes and details →

Jackson's Grant on Williams Creek

Best for: luxury buyers and buyers who want built-in amenities.

Jackson's Grant on Williams Creek is a luxury master-planned community in Carmel with homes typically in the $1M to $2M+. Schools: Carmel Clay Schools.

  • 280 acres along Williams Creek at 116th St & Spring Mill Rd
  • Two-story clubhouse with three outdoor pools, fitness center and tennis
  • Wooded preserves, trails and the preserved Hobby Horse Barn
  • Custom homes built to a pattern book by several builders

See Jackson's Grant homes and details →

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Carmel

What are the best neighborhoods in Carmel, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Carmel’s neighborhoods range from Village of Mount Carmel ($200s to $500s) to Crooked Stick Estates ($800s to $3M+); for new construction, look at The Sanctuary at 116th; for golf-course living, Brookshire and Plum Creek; for waterfront living, Woodland Springs. We group all 17 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Carmel?

Of the 17 neighborhoods in this guide, Village of Mount Carmel has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $500s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Carmel neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, The Sanctuary at 116th is new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Carmel?

Yes. The Sanctuary at 116th, Crooked Stick Estates and Jackson's Grant are among the Carmel neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Carmel neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Carmel Clay Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
