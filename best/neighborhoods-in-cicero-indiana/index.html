Best Neighborhoods in Cicero, Indiana

7 Cicero neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

Cicero sits in Hamilton County, and like most Central Indiana towns it’s really a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range, age of homes and amenities.

This guide covers the 7 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Cicero that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Established Family Neighborhoods

Settled subdivisions with mature trees, neighborhood amenities and a track record.

Crystal Lake

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Crystal Lake is a small established subdivision in Cicero with homes typically in the $200s to $400s. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • Small community of roughly 30 homesites
  • Over 7 acres of common area with play area and basketball court
  • East of Hague Road near the Morse marina

See Crystal Lake homes and details →

Morse Landing

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Morse Landing is an established subdivision in Cicero with homes typically in the mid $300s to $600s. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • Upscale west-side community near Morse Reservoir
  • Larger single-family homes on landscaped lots
  • Popular family neighborhood close to lake recreation

See Morse Landing homes and details →

Stillwater Cove

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Stillwater Cove is an upscale subdivision in Cicero with homes typically in the upper $300s to $600s. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • Upscale development on the west side of Morse Reservoir
  • A move-up home area near the lake
  • Single-family homes minutes from the water and marina

See Stillwater Cove homes and details →

Lake & Waterfront Neighborhoods

Homes on or near the water, from private lakes to reservoir frontage.

Waterfront Condominiums at Morse Lake

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water and buyers who want less upkeep.

Waterfront Condominiums at Morse Lake is a lakefront condo building in Cicero with homes typically in the $580s to $630s. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • 2006 lakefront condo building at 420 W. Jackson St. on Morse Reservoir
  • One-level 2-bed, 2-bath flats (about 1,750 sq ft) with elevator and garage parking
  • Private pool and boat slips at the building's dock
  • Walkable to downtown Cicero; Hamilton Heights schools

See Waterfront Condos at Morse homes and details →

Luxury & Estate Neighborhoods

The top of the local market: custom and estate homes, larger lots, club amenities.

Forest Bay

Best for: luxury buyers.

Forest Bay is an estate subdivision in Cicero with homes typically in the upper $400s to $800s. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • Estate homes in a wooded setting near Morse Reservoir
  • Larger lots and privacy
  • Served by Hamilton Heights schools

See Forest Bay homes and details →

Calumet Farms

Best for: luxury buyers.

Calumet Farms is an estate subdivision in Cicero with homes typically in the $500s to $900s. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • Estate homes on larger lots in a rural setting
  • A spacious alternative to lakefront living
  • Cicero-area community in Hamilton Heights schools

See Calumet Farms homes and details →

Bear Slide

Best for: luxury buyers and golfers.

Bear Slide is an estate golf-adjacent community in Cicero with homes typically in the $500s to $1M+. Schools: Hamilton Heights School Corporation.

  • Estate homes on larger lots in a rural setting
  • Adjacent to Bear Slide Golf Club
  • Secluded feel within the Cicero area

See Bear Slide homes and details →

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Cicero

What are the best neighborhoods in Cicero, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Cicero’s neighborhoods range from Crystal Lake ($200s to $400s) to Bear Slide ($500s to $1M+); for waterfront living, Waterfront Condos at Morse. We group all 7 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Cicero?

Of the 7 neighborhoods in this guide, Crystal Lake has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $400s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Cicero?

Yes. Forest Bay, Calumet Farms and Bear Slide are among the Cicero neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Cicero neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Hamilton Heights School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Cicero neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Cicero, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
