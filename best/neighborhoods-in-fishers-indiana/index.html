Best Neighborhoods in Fishers, Indiana

16 Fishers neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hamilton County 🏡 16 neighborhoods 💰 $250s – $1.5M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Fishers’ named neighborhoods run from Heritage Meadows (typically $250s to $400s) to Masthead at Geist ($700s to $1.5M+). For new construction, look at Cyntheanne Meadows; for a master-planned community, Saxony; for golf-course living, Windermere and Hamilton Proper; and for less upkeep, Britton Falls. Most are served by Hamilton Southeastern Schools.

Fishers is one of America's most-recognized 'best places to live' — family-friendly and anchored by a lively Nickel Plate District with its amphitheater and farmers market. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-69 and SR-37, and homes across Fishers typically range from the low $300s to the $600s and beyond.

This guide covers the 16 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Fishers that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Fishers

What are the best neighborhoods in Fishers, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Fishers’ neighborhoods range from Heritage Meadows ($250s to $400s) to Masthead at Geist ($700s to $1.5M+); for new construction, look at Cyntheanne Meadows; for golf-course living, Windermere and Hamilton Proper. We group all 16 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Fishers?

Of the 16 neighborhoods in this guide, Heritage Meadows has the lowest typical price range, about $250s to $400s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Fishers neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Cyntheanne Meadows is new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Fishers?

Yes. Masthead at Geist, Windermere and Hamilton Proper are among the Fishers neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Fishers neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Hamilton Southeastern Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
