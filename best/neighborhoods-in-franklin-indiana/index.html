Best Neighborhoods in Franklin, Indiana

8 Franklin neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Johnson County 🏡 8 neighborhoods 💰 $150s – $1M+

Quick Answer

Franklin’s named neighborhoods run from Heritage at Brookhaven (typically $150s to $300s) to Homesteads at Hillview ($400s to $1M+). For new construction, look at Bluffs at Young's Creek and Westwind at Cumberland, and for golf-course living, Legends West and Homesteads at Hillview. Most are served by Franklin Community School Corporation.

Franklin is the Johnson County seat, with a beautifully restored courthouse-square downtown, the historic Artcraft Theatre, and a genuine small-town college-town feel. It’s about 30 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-65 and US-31, and homes across Franklin typically range from the $200s to the $400s.

This guide covers the 8 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Franklin that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Franklin

What are the best neighborhoods in Franklin, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Franklin’s neighborhoods range from Heritage at Brookhaven ($150s to $300s) to Homesteads at Hillview ($400s to $1M+); for new construction, look at Bluffs at Young's Creek and Westwind at Cumberland; for golf-course living, Legends West and Homesteads at Hillview. We group all 8 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Franklin?

Of the 8 neighborhoods in this guide, Heritage at Brookhaven has the lowest typical price range, about $150s to $300s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Franklin neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Bluffs at Young's Creek and Westwind at Cumberland are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Franklin?

Yes. Legends West and Homesteads at Hillview are among the Franklin neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Franklin neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Franklin Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Franklin neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Franklin, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
