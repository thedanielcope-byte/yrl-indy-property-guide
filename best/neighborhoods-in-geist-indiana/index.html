Best Neighborhoods in Geist, Indiana

9 Geist neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Marion County 🏡 9 neighborhoods 💰 $300s – $2M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Geist’s named neighborhoods run from Sail Place (typically $300s to $700s) to Masthead ($550s to $2M+). For the water, Sail Place and Windjammer. Most are served by MSD of Lawrence Township or Hamilton Southeastern Schools.

Geist sits in Marion County, and like most Central Indiana towns it’s really a collection of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own price range, age of homes and amenities.

This guide covers the 9 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Geist that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Lake & Waterfront Neighborhoods

Homes on or near the water, from private lakes to reservoir frontage.

Sail Place

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Sail Place is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $300s to $700s. Schools: Hamilton Southeastern Schools.

  • Small Geist Reservoir community of about 30 lots, many with deeded docks
  • Hamilton County side of Geist, near the marinas
  • HOA handles lawn care and snow removal

See Sail Place homes and details →

Windjammer at Geist

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Windjammer at Geist is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $300s to $700s. Schools: Hamilton Southeastern Schools.

  • Wooded Geist-area subdivision on the Hamilton County side
  • Minutes from Geist Reservoir boating and fishing
  • Near Geist shopping and dining

See Windjammer homes and details →

Admirals Bay

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Admirals Bay is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the mid $300s to $800s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Part of the Admirals cluster in the Geist Harbours POA
  • Mix of waterfront and water-access interior homes
  • Boating, swimming and marina amenities

See Admirals Bay homes and details →

Diamond Pointe

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Diamond Pointe is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $400s to $800s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Established Geist Harbours POA neighborhood
  • Water-access and waterfront homesites
  • Nature trails and boating access

See Diamond Pointe homes and details →

Admirals Pointe

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Admirals Pointe is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $400s to $900s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • One of the four Admirals subdivisions in the POA
  • Peninsula-style lots with reservoir frontage
  • Shared POA marina and water recreation

See Admirals Pointe homes and details →

The Anchorage

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

The Anchorage is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $400s to $900s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Established waterfront subdivision on the Marion County side of Geist
  • Mature, wooded reservoir lots
  • Docks and direct lake access

See The Anchorage homes and details →

Feather Cove

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Feather Cove is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the mid $400s to $900s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Sits on the main body of Geist Reservoir
  • Three phases within the Geist Harbours POA
  • Established waterfront homes with docks

See Feather Cove homes and details →

Masthead at Geist

Best for: buyers who want to live on the water.

Masthead at Geist is a waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $550s to $2M+. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township or Hamilton Southeastern Schools (it straddles the county line; confirm by address).

  • One of the first two Geist Reservoir subdivisions (construction began in 1980)
  • Custom homes on the main body of Geist, many waterfront with docks
  • Straddles the Marion/Hamilton line: Lawrence Township or HSE schools by address
  • Association dues about $525 a year (listings cite Geist Harbours POA)

See Masthead homes and details →

Luxury & Estate Neighborhoods

The top of the local market: custom and estate homes, larger lots, club amenities.

Bridgewater at Geist

Best for: luxury buyers and buyers who want to live on the water.

Bridgewater at Geist is a luxury waterfront community in Geist with homes typically in the $800s to $1.5M+. Schools: Hamilton Southeastern Schools.

  • Premier wooded enclave with half-acre-plus custom estate lots
  • Many homes with private boat docks
  • Indianapolis mailing address but served by Hamilton Southeastern Schools

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Geist

What are the best neighborhoods in Geist, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Geist’s neighborhoods range from Sail Place ($300s to $700s) to Masthead ($550s to $2M+); for waterfront living, Sail Place and Windjammer. We group all 9 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Geist?

Of the 9 neighborhoods in this guide, Sail Place has the lowest typical price range, about $300s to $700s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Geist?

Yes. Bridgewater is among the Geist neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Geist neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by MSD of Lawrence Township and Hamilton Southeastern Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Geist neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Geist, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
