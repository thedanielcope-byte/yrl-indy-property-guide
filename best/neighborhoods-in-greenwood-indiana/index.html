Best Neighborhoods in Greenwood, Indiana

14 Greenwood neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Johnson County 🏡 14 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $700s

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Greenwood’s named neighborhoods run from Foxberry Trace (typically $200s to $300s) to Maxwell Springs ($400s to $700s). For new construction, look at Lone Pine Farms, Scottsdale Estates and Berry Chase; for a master-planned community, Villages at Grassy Creek; for golf-course living, Cobblestone; for the water, Willow Lakes; and for less upkeep, The Retreat and Sagebriar. Most are served by Center Grove Community Schools or Clark-Pleasant Community School Corporation.

Greenwood is the south metro's fast-growing retail and dining hub, offering strong value, an active Old Town, and easy interstate access. It’s around 15 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-65 and US-31, and homes across Greenwood typically range from the $250s to the $450s.

This guide covers the 14 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Greenwood that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Greenwood

What are the best neighborhoods in Greenwood, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Greenwood’s neighborhoods range from Foxberry Trace ($200s to $300s) to Maxwell Springs ($400s to $700s); for new construction, look at Lone Pine Farms, Scottsdale Estates and Berry Chase; for golf-course living, Cobblestone; for waterfront living, Willow Lakes. We group all 14 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Greenwood?

Of the 14 neighborhoods in this guide, Foxberry Trace has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $300s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Greenwood neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Lone Pine Farms, Scottsdale Estates, Berry Chase and Brighton Knoll are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Greenwood?

Yes. Maxwell Springs and Cobblestone are among the Greenwood neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Greenwood neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Center Grove Community Schools and Clark-Pleasant Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Greenwood neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Greenwood, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
