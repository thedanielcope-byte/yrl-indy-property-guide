Best Neighborhoods in Lawrence, Indiana

8 Lawrence neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Marion County 🏡 8 neighborhoods 💰 $150s – $700s+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Lawrence’s named neighborhoods run from Lawrence Woods (typically $150s to $280s) to Oakland Hills at Geist ($360s to $700s+). For new construction, look at Silver Stream; for a master-planned community, Fort Ben; and for golf-course living, Winding Ridge. Most are served by MSD of Lawrence Township.

Lawrence is a northeast-side city built around historic Fort Harrison State Park and the Fort Ben cultural campus, with affordable homes and green space. It’s about 20 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-465 and Pendleton Pike, and homes across Lawrence typically range from the $150s to the $320s.

This guide covers the 8 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Lawrence that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Established Family Neighborhoods

Settled subdivisions with mature trees, neighborhood amenities and a track record.

Lawrence Woods

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Lawrence Woods is an established subdivision in Lawrence with homes typically in the $150s to $280s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • single-family homes on mature, tree-lined lots
  • convenient to Pendleton Pike shopping
  • quick access to I-465 and I-70

See Lawrence Woods homes and details →

Oaklandon

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Oaklandon is an established neighborhood in Lawrence with homes typically in the low-$200s to $400s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • historic hamlet in northeast Lawrence Township
  • mix of older homes and newer subdivisions
  • close to Geist Reservoir recreation

See Oaklandon homes and details →

Kensington Farms

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Kensington Farms is an established family subdivision in Lawrence with homes typically in the $210s to $390s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Single-family homes built in the mid-to-late 1990s
  • Pool, playground, basketball court and walking trails
  • Registered HOA in Lawrence’s Sunnyside neighborhood

See Kensington Farms homes and details →

Watson Farms

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Watson Farms is an established family subdivision in Lawrence with homes typically in the $250s to $380s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • 327 homes on a former family dairy farm, developed from 1993
  • Heated pool and bathhouse, plus five retention ponds
  • Between 59th and 63rd streets, near Fort Harrison State Park

See Watson Farms homes and details →

Oakland Hills at Geist

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Oakland Hills at Geist is an established family subdivision in Lawrence with homes typically in the $360s to $700s+. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Larger homes built from the late 1980s through the 2010s
  • Near the private Old Oakland Golf Club on East 75th Street
  • Inside the City of Lawrence, a short drive south of Geist Reservoir

See Oakland Hills at Geist homes and details →

New-Construction Communities

Communities where builders are, or recently were, still building, so you can buy new or nearly new.

Silver Stream

Best for: buyers who want a new or nearly new home.

Silver Stream is a new-construction community in Lawrence with homes typically in the $240s to $360s. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • New Arbor Homes single-family homes, 3–5 bedrooms
  • Ponds, walking trails, playground and dog park
  • Inside the City of Lawrence, in the township’s southeast

See Silver Stream homes and details →

Master-Planned Communities

Larger planned communities with shared amenities designed in from the start.

Fort Harrison

Best for: buyers who want built-in amenities.

Fort Harrison is a master-planned community in Lawrence with homes typically in the $220s to $700s+. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • Early-1900s officers' quarters on Lawton Loop, now single-family homes and condos
  • Newer single-family homes and townhomes, including Davis Homes' Towns at Fort Harrison
  • Next to the roughly 1,700-acre Fort Harrison State Park
  • MSD of Lawrence Township schools

See Fort Ben homes and details →

Golf Communities

Neighborhoods built around a golf course, with fairway and club-adjacent homes.

Winding Ridge

Best for: golfers.

Winding Ridge is a golf community in Lawrence with homes typically in the mid-$200s to $400s+. Schools: MSD of Lawrence Township.

  • 18-hole public golf course & clubhouse
  • community pool, tennis & basketball courts
  • ponds and walking trails, minutes to I-465

See Winding Ridge homes and details →

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Lawrence

What are the best neighborhoods in Lawrence, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Lawrence’s neighborhoods range from Lawrence Woods ($150s to $280s) to Oakland Hills at Geist ($360s to $700s+); for new construction, look at Silver Stream; for golf-course living, Winding Ridge. We group all 8 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Lawrence?

Of the 8 neighborhoods in this guide, Lawrence Woods has the lowest typical price range, about $150s to $280s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Lawrence neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Silver Stream is new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Lawrence?

Yes. Winding Ridge is among the Lawrence neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Lawrence neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by MSD of Lawrence Township. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Lawrence neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Lawrence, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
