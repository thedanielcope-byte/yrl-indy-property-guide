Best Neighborhoods in Lebanon, Indiana

8 Lebanon neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Boone County 🏡 8 neighborhoods 💰 $160s – $400s

Quick Answer

Lebanon’s named neighborhoods run from Prairie Heights (typically $160s to $250s) to Cedar Ridge ($300s to $400s). For new construction, look at Cedar Ridge, Auburn Meadows and Sunbrook. Most are served by Lebanon Community School Corporation.

Lebanon is the Boone County seat with a classic courthouse-square downtown, affordable homes, and major growth on the horizon from the nearby LEAP innovation district. It’s about 35 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-65, and homes across Lebanon typically range from the $230s to the $400s.

This guide covers the 8 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Lebanon that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Lebanon

What are the best neighborhoods in Lebanon, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Lebanon’s neighborhoods range from Prairie Heights ($160s to $250s) to Cedar Ridge ($300s to $400s); for new construction, look at Cedar Ridge, Auburn Meadows and Sunbrook. We group all 8 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Lebanon?

Of the 8 neighborhoods in this guide, Prairie Heights has the lowest typical price range, about $160s to $250s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Lebanon neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Cedar Ridge, Auburn Meadows, Sunbrook and Angilee Gardens are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

What school district serves Lebanon neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Lebanon Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Lebanon neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Lebanon, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
