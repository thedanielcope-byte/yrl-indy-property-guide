Best Neighborhoods in McCordsville, Indiana

11 McCordsville neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hancock County 🏡 11 neighborhoods 💰 $280s – $1M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

McCordsville’s named neighborhoods run from Bay Creek (typically $280s to $500s) to Hampton Cove ($700s to $1M+). For new construction, look at Emerald Springs, Haven Ponds and Colonnade; for a master-planned community, Gateway Crossing and McCord Pointe; and for less upkeep, Gatherings at Aurora. Most are served by Mt. Vernon Community School Corporation.

McCordsville is Hancock County's fastest-growing town, offering newer construction, strong schools, and a brand-new walkable town center at McCord Square. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-69 and Fall Creek Road, and homes across McCordsville typically range from the $280s to the $500s.

This guide covers the 11 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in McCordsville that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Established Family Neighborhoods

Settled subdivisions with mature trees, neighborhood amenities and a track record.

Bay Creek

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Bay Creek is an established family subdivision in McCordsville with homes typically in the $280s to $500s.

  • Established neighborhood near Geist
  • Well-maintained homes
  • Mix of young families & retirees

Villages at Brookside

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Villages at Brookside is a single-family subdivision in McCordsville with homes typically in the $350s to $500s. Schools: Mt. Vernon Community School Corporation.

  • Fischer Homes community, now sold out (resale only)
  • Neighborhood pool, playground, basketball court and walking trails

55+ Active-Adult Communities

Age-restricted communities built for buyers 55 and older.

Gatherings at Aurora

Best for: buyers 55 and older.

Gatherings at Aurora is a 55+ active-adult community in McCordsville with homes typically in the $400s to $500s. Schools: Mt. Vernon Community School Corporation.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in McCordsville

What are the best neighborhoods in McCordsville, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, McCordsville’s neighborhoods range from Bay Creek ($280s to $500s) to Hampton Cove ($700s to $1M+); for new construction, look at Emerald Springs, Haven Ponds and Colonnade. We group all 11 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in McCordsville?

Of the 11 neighborhoods in this guide, Bay Creek has the lowest typical price range, about $280s to $500s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which McCordsville neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Emerald Springs, Haven Ponds, Colonnade and Weavers Landing are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in McCordsville?

Yes. Hampton Cove is among the McCordsville neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves McCordsville neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Mt. Vernon Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific McCordsville neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by McCordsville, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
