Best Neighborhoods in Mooresville, Indiana

8 Mooresville neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Morgan County 🏡 8 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $600s

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Mooresville’s named neighborhoods run from Bridgemor Village (typically $200s to $300s) to Country View Estates ($300s to $600s). For a master-planned community, Heartland Crossing, and for less upkeep, Bridgemor Village. Most are served by Mooresville Consolidated School Corporation.

Mooresville is a friendly southwest-metro town with small-town charm, a walkable downtown, strong schools, and quick access to the interstate. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via SR-67 and I-70, and homes across Mooresville typically range from the $200s to the $380s.

This guide covers the 8 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Mooresville that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Mooresville

What are the best neighborhoods in Mooresville, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Mooresville’s neighborhoods range from Bridgemor Village ($200s to $300s) to Country View Estates ($300s to $600s). We group all 8 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Mooresville?

Of the 8 neighborhoods in this guide, Bridgemor Village has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $300s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

What school district serves Mooresville neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Mooresville Consolidated School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
