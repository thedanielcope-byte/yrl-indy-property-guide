Best Neighborhoods in Noblesville, Indiana

16 Noblesville neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hamilton County 🏡 16 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $1M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Noblesville’s named neighborhoods run from The Meadows (typically $200s to $350s) to Sagamore ($400s to $1M+). For new construction, look at Slater Ridge, Magnolia Ridge and Parkside at Finch Creek; for a master-planned community, Lochaven and Prairie Crossing; for golf-course living, North Harbour and Sagamore; for the water, South Harbour; and for less upkeep, The Retreat at Morse and Courtyards of Hazel Dell. Most are served by Noblesville Schools or Hamilton Southeastern Schools.

Noblesville is Hamilton County's historic county seat, blending a charming courthouse-square downtown with lakefront living and steady new growth. It’s about 30 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via SR-37 and I-69, and homes across Noblesville typically range from the $300s to the $600s.

This guide covers the 16 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Noblesville that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Noblesville

What are the best neighborhoods in Noblesville, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Noblesville’s neighborhoods range from The Meadows ($200s to $350s) to Sagamore ($400s to $1M+); for new construction, look at Slater Ridge, Magnolia Ridge and Parkside at Finch Creek; for golf-course living, North Harbour and Sagamore; for waterfront living, South Harbour. We group all 16 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Noblesville?

Of the 16 neighborhoods in this guide, The Meadows has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $350s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Noblesville neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Slater Ridge, Magnolia Ridge and Parkside at Finch Creek are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

Yes. North Harbour and Sagamore are among the Noblesville neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Noblesville Schools and Hamilton Southeastern Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
