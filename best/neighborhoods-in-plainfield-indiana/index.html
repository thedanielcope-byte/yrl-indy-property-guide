Best Neighborhoods in Plainfield, Indiana

10 Plainfield neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hendricks County 🏡 10 neighborhoods 💰 $230s – $700s+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Plainfield’s named neighborhoods run from Saratoga (typically $230s to $400s) to Williamsburg in the Woods ($550s to $700s+). For new construction, look at Grey Hawk, Trescott Gardens and Fairwood, and for a master-planned community, Saratoga. Most are served by Plainfield Community School Corporation.

Plainfield is a thriving Hendricks County town known for the Metropolis shopping district, excellent parks and trails, and easy interstate and airport access. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-70 and US-40, and homes across Plainfield typically range from the $230s to the $400s.

This guide covers the 10 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Plainfield that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

New-Construction Communities

Communities where builders are, or recently were, still building, so you can buy new or nearly new.

Fairwood is a new-construction community in Plainfield with homes typically in the $400s to $500s.

  • Oversized homesites with ponds and mature trees

  • Assigned to the Avon school district (not Plainfield)

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Plainfield

What are the best neighborhoods in Plainfield, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Plainfield’s neighborhoods range from Saratoga ($230s to $400s) to Williamsburg in the Woods ($550s to $700s+); for new construction, look at Grey Hawk, Trescott Gardens and Fairwood. We group all 10 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Plainfield?

Of the 10 neighborhoods in this guide, Saratoga has the lowest typical price range, about $230s to $400s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Plainfield neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Grey Hawk, Trescott Gardens and Fairwood are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

What school district serves Plainfield neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Plainfield Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
