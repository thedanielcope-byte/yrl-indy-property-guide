Best Neighborhoods in Shelbyville, Indiana

6 Shelbyville neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

Quick Answer

Shelbyville’s named neighborhoods run from Fountain Lake (typically $150s to $300s) to Clearview ($300s to $400s). For new construction, look at Bear Run, Twelve Oaks and Isabelle Farms, and for the water, Fountain Lake. Most are served by Shelbyville Central Schools.

Shelbyville is the Shelby County seat offering some of the metro's most affordable homes, a revitalizing downtown on the Blue River, and easy interstate access. It’s about 35 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-74, and homes across Shelbyville typically range from the $150s to the $300s.

This guide covers the 6 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Shelbyville that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Shelbyville

What are the best neighborhoods in Shelbyville, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Shelbyville’s neighborhoods range from Fountain Lake ($150s to $300s) to Clearview ($300s to $400s); for new construction, look at Bear Run, Twelve Oaks and Isabelle Farms; for waterfront living, Fountain Lake. We group all 6 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Shelbyville?

Of the 6 neighborhoods in this guide, Fountain Lake has the lowest typical price range, about $150s to $300s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Shelbyville neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, Bear Run, Twelve Oaks, Isabelle Farms, Summerfield and Clearview are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

What school district serves Shelbyville neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Shelbyville Central Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.

How do I search homes in a specific Shelbyville neighborhood?

Use our MIBOR listing search and filter by Shelbyville, or tell us which neighborhoods you like and we’ll set up alerts and tours. Every neighborhood page in this guide also links to its listings.
