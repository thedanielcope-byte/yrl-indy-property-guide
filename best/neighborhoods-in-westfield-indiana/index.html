Best Neighborhoods in Westfield, Indiana

16 Westfield neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Hamilton County 🏡 16 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $2M+

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Westfield’s named neighborhoods run from Villages of Oak Manor (typically $200s to $400s) to Chatham Hills ($500s to $2M+). For a master-planned community, Harmony and Viking Meadows; for golf-course living, Wood Wind; and for less upkeep, Villages of Oak Manor and Centennial. Most are served by Westfield Washington Schools.

Westfield is home of the massive Grand Park Sports Campus and one of Indiana's fastest-growing cities, with a rapidly developing downtown. It’s roughly 30 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via US-31, and homes across Westfield typically range from the $300s to the $600s.

This guide covers the 16 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Westfield that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Westfield

What are the best neighborhoods in Westfield, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Westfield’s neighborhoods range from Villages of Oak Manor ($200s to $400s) to Chatham Hills ($500s to $2M+); for golf-course living, Wood Wind. We group all 16 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Westfield?

Of the 16 neighborhoods in this guide, Villages of Oak Manor has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $400s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Are there luxury or golf neighborhoods in Westfield?

Yes. Chatham Hills, The Bridgewater Club and Brookside are among the Westfield neighborhoods in our guide with luxury, estate or golf-course homes. Each has its own page with typical prices and amenities.

What school district serves Westfield neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Westfield Washington Schools. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
