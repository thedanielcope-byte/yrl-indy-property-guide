Best Neighborhoods in Whitestown, Indiana

11 Whitestown neighborhoods and subdivisions, grouped by what buyers usually want, with typical price ranges, schools and what each one is best for.

📍 Boone County 🏡 11 neighborhoods 💰 $200s – $500s

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated November 2026

Quick Answer

Whitestown’s named neighborhoods run from Townhomes at Anson (typically $200s to $300s) to Jackson Run ($350s to $500s). For new construction, look at The Highlands, Jackson Run and Windswept Farms; for a master-planned community, Walker Farms and The Neighborhoods at Anson; and for less upkeep, Townhomes at Anson and Ellis Acres. Most are served by Lebanon Community School Corporation.

Whitestown is one of the fastest-growing towns in Indiana, anchored by the Anson mixed-use development, new schools, and quick interstate access. It’s about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-65, and homes across Whitestown typically range from the $250s to the $450s.

This guide covers the 11 named neighborhoods and subdivisions in Whitestown that we track in our Central Indiana communities directory. They’re grouped by the kind of home and lifestyle buyers usually ask us for, not ranked, because the best neighborhood depends on your budget, your schools and how you want to live. Each one links to its own page with more detail.

How We Chose These

Every neighborhood here is a real, named subdivision or community, researched from builder, HOA, county and listing sources for our communities directory. We group them by type (established, new construction, golf, waterfront, luxury and so on) instead of ranking them, since a great fit for a first-time buyer and a great fit for an estate-home buyer are different places.

Price ranges are typical, rounded ranges, not appraisals; any single home can fall outside them. School districts are the district most of each neighborhood falls in, but boundaries can split a subdivision, so always confirm the assignment for a specific address. Nothing here is sponsored.

Established Family Neighborhoods

Settled subdivisions with mature trees, neighborhood amenities and a track record.

Edmonds Creek at Anson

Best for: families who want an established neighborhood.

Edmonds Creek at Anson is a single-family subdivision in Whitestown with homes typically in the $300s to low $400s. Schools: Lebanon Community School Corporation.

  • Arbor Homes neighborhood within Whitestown's Anson development
  • Two phases: Edmonds Creek and Edmonds Creek North
  • Single-family plans of roughly 1,600-3,200 sq ft, some with pond views
  • Lebanon Community School Corporation

Frequently Asked Questions — Neighborhoods in Whitestown

What are the best neighborhoods in Whitestown, Indiana?

It depends on what you want. In our guide, Whitestown’s neighborhoods range from Townhomes at Anson ($200s to $300s) to Jackson Run ($350s to $500s); for new construction, look at The Highlands, Jackson Run and Windswept Farms. We group all 11 by type above, with typical prices and schools for each.

What is the most affordable neighborhood in Whitestown?

Of the 11 neighborhoods in this guide, Townhomes at Anson has the lowest typical price range, about $200s to $300s. Prices vary by home, so ask us for a current market analysis on any address.

Which Whitestown neighborhoods have new construction?

In our guide, The Highlands, Jackson Run, Windswept Farms and Bridle Oaks are new or newer construction. Builder inventory changes quickly, so check with us for which sections are still building.

What school district serves Whitestown neighborhoods?

Most of the neighborhoods in this guide are served by Lebanon Community School Corporation. Boundaries can split a subdivision, so confirm the school assignment for a specific address with the district.
