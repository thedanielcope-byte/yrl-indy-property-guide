Do Open Houses Actually Sell Homes? An Honest Look

The straight answer for Indianapolis sellers — what an open house really does, and what actually gets your home sold.

Selling a Home Seller Strategy Central Indiana
Selling a Home August 2026 By Daniel Cope, Real Estate Broker — Your Realty Link ~7 min read

Every seller asks it, and every honest agent should give a straight answer: does an open house actually sell your house? The short version is that a single open house rarely closes the deal by itself — but open houses, done right, are still a valuable piece of a bigger plan. Let us separate the myth from what really moves a home in Indianapolis, so you can decide how open houses fit your sale. When you are ready, here is how we market and sell homes across Central Indiana.

The Honest Reality

Most people who attend an open house are early in their search — and some are simply curious neighbors. It is uncommon for the exact buyer to walk in off the street during those two hours and write an offer that day. So if you are judging an open house purely by did it produce a buyer this afternoon, it will usually look like it did not.

But that is the wrong scoreboard. Open houses are a marketing tool, not a closing table. Their job is to expand the pool of people who experience your home and to build momentum in the critical first days on market — and on that scoreboard, they can absolutely earn their keep.

What Open Houses Actually Do Well

A well-run open house creates buzz right when it matters most: launch week. It gives neighbors a reason to tell friends who are looking, gives on-the-fence buyers a low-pressure way to take a first look, and signals confidence in the home. Feedback from visitors can also be gold — if ten people love the kitchen but flinch at the price, that tells you something fast.

And a modern open house is not only in-person. Offering a virtual, live-video option lets serious out-of-area buyers experience your home when they can not attend in person — which matters a lot for the relocation buyers moving to Central Indiana for work who might otherwise skip your listing entirely.

What Actually Gets Your Home Sold

If open houses are one instrument, here is the rest of the orchestra. What truly sells a home is the whole package: correct pricing from day one, strong staging and professional photography, full MIBOR MLS exposure so every agent and portal has it, and an agent who markets it everywhere buyers are actually looking. Get those right and the open house amplifies a listing that was already positioned to win.

Get them wrong — especially pricing — and no number of open houses will fix it. The market is honest; a great open house can not rescue an overpriced home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I have an open house when I sell my Indianapolis home?

In most cases it is worth doing, ideally during launch week, as part of a broader marketing plan. It builds early momentum and widens exposure. Just do not expect it to sell the home on its own — pricing, presentation, and full MLS marketing do the heavy lifting.

Are open houses safe for sellers?

With sensible precautions they are. Put away valuables, medications, and sensitive documents, and make sure the home is hosted by an agent, never left open unattended. Your Realty Link manages the details so your home is shown responsibly.

Can you do a virtual open house for my listing?

Yes. Alongside a traditional open house, we can offer private live-video showings to serious buyers who can not attend in person — a real advantage for reaching out-of-town and relocation buyers.
