First-Time Home Buyer Tips How to Start Strong in Central Indiana

From pre-approval to your first showing, here's the path we walk every new buyer through.

Home Buying First-Time Buyers Central Indiana

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated August 2026

Buying a Home August 2026 By Daniel Cope, Real Estate Broker — Your Realty Link ~6 min read

Buying your first home is exciting, and a little overwhelming. There are new terms, big numbers, and a process nobody really teaches you. Take it one step at a time and it's very doable. At Your Realty Link we walk first-time buyers through it every day across Central Indiana, and we've put together a fuller guide for first-time home buyers in Indianapolis. Here are the fundamentals to get you moving.

Get Pre-Approved First

Before you fall in love with a listing, talk to a lender and get pre-approved. Pre-approval tells you what you can comfortably afford, shows sellers you're a serious buyer, and prevents the heartbreak of finding the perfect home outside your range.

If you don't have a lender, ask us. We work with trusted local lenders who can match you to the right type of loan for your situation, whether that's conventional, FHA, VA, or a first-time buyer program. A good lender will also walk you through your monthly payment so there are no surprises.

Search Smart, Not Just a Lot

Nearly every buyer starts online today, and that's a great place to begin. Use the property search at yourrealtylink.com to browse current listings across Central Indiana, and set up alerts so new homes that match your criteria land in your inbox the moment they hit the market.

Those alerts do double duty: they keep you first in line in a fast market, and they let your agent see which homes are catching your eye so we can fine-tune the search. Great photos and video tours also help you narrow the list before you spend a Saturday driving around.

Tour With a Plan

Once you're pre-approved and something catches your attention, we'll schedule showings. Walking through in person tells you things photos never will: how the light feels, how the layout flows, and what the neighborhood sounds like on a weekday evening.

Try to see homes while your financing is in place so you can act quickly when the right one shows up. Take notes and photos as you go, because after three or four homes they start to blur together, and your notes help you compare them fairly later.

Understand Who Pays What

One of the most common first-time-buyer questions is simply, how does this all work? We'll sit down with you and walk through a buyer's guide that explains each step, from offer to inspection to closing, and who's responsible for what along the way, including how buyer agency and compensation work in Indiana.

There's no such thing as a silly question here, and there's no pressure. Our job is to make sure you feel informed and confident before you sign anything. Ready to start? Call Your Realty Link at 317-997-7404 and we'll help you take the first step.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on your loan. Some programs allow low down payments, and there are down payment assistance options in Indiana. A local lender can show you exactly what fits your budget.

Should I get pre-approved before I start looking?

Yes. Pre-approval clarifies your budget and makes your offers stronger. In a competitive market, many sellers won't seriously consider an offer without it.

Does it cost anything to work with a buyer's agent?

Buyer agency involves a written agreement that spells out how your agent is paid, and the details vary by transaction. We'll explain your options clearly and up front before you commit to anything.

How long does buying a home take?

From pre-approval to closing, it often runs a couple of months, though it varies with your search and financing. Being ready to move quickly on the right home helps a lot.
