Living in Zionsville at a Glance

Is Zionsville, Indiana a good place to live? For buyers who put schools and small-town character first, Zionsville is a standout: a brick-paved Village, Zionsville Community Schools, wooded lots and the Big-4 Rail Trail. It’s also among the metro’s priciest towns, with homes from the mid-$400s to over $1 million, and Boone County’s income tax runs higher than Hamilton County’s. Downtown Indianapolis is about 25 minutes away.

Zionsville is small-town luxury: a brick-paved Main Street, wooded lots and Zionsville Community Schools, among the state’s top-rated. It’s also one of the most expensive places to buy in the metro, and Boone County’s local income tax went up on October 1, 2026.

Pros of Living in Zionsville

  • The brick-paved Main Street Village offers boutique shopping and dining in a genuine small-town setting.
  • Zionsville Community Schools are among the state’s top-rated.
  • The paved Big-4 Rail Trail links the Village to nearby neighborhoods and parks.
  • Wooded estates and golf living at Holliday Farms, about 25 minutes from downtown via I-65 and Michigan Road.

Cons of Living in Zionsville

  • Homes run from the mid-$400s to over $1 million, and even with Indiana’s 1% homestead tax cap, bills grow with home price.
  • Boone County’s local income tax rose to 1.71% on October 1, 2026, versus 1.10% in Hamilton County — roughly $610 more a year per $100,000 of income.
  • Growth is pressing in: neighboring Whitestown grew about 53% from 2020 to 2025, and INDOT’s “421 Forward” plan calls for rebuilding Michigan Road.

Taxes. Boone County’s 1.71% local income tax (2026) is below Marion County’s 2.02%, about $310 a year less per $100,000 of income; Boone County’s rate rose from 1.70% to 1.71% on October 1, 2026, though Hamilton County’s 1.10% is lower still. On property taxes, Indiana caps the bill on your primary home at 1% of its gross assessed value (not counting voter-approved referendums), and the homestead deduction lowers the taxable value, but only if it’s filed. Our homestead exemption guide explains how it works, and property taxes by county compares the metro.

Neighborhoods and Communities in Zionsville

Popular areas include the historic brick-street Village, the Holliday Farms golf community, Stonegate and Austin Oaks and wooded estates on the north and west sides.

We profile 14 Zionsville communities, including Holliday Farms, Irishman's Run, Stonegate, The Willows, Austin Oaks, Royal Run, Cobblestone Lakes and Colony Woods. Browse them all in our communities directory.

Schools in Zionsville

All of Zionsville is served by Zionsville Community Schools.

Public school districts: Zionsville Community Schools.

School assignment follows the address, so confirm boundaries with the district before you write an offer. Our Central Indiana schools guide covers every district.

Commute and Getting Around

Zionsville is about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis via I-65 and Michigan Road. Rush-hour times run longer, so test-drive the commute at the hours you’d actually drive it. Comparing drives? See the best Indianapolis suburbs for commuters.

Things to Do in Zionsville

Here’s a sample of what fills the Zionsville calendar through the year:

  • Zionsville Fall Festival (late summer (Labor Day weekend)): The Zionsville Fall Festival runs over Labor Day weekend at Lions Park with a carnival, food vendors, community booths, and a Saturday parade through the Village.
  • Brick Street Market (spring (May)): The Brick Street Market lines Zionsville's brick-paved Main Street each May with well over a hundred artisan and craft vendors.
  • Zionsville Farmers Market (summer): The Zionsville Farmers Market runs Saturday mornings through the growing season in the historic Village.

Event calendars: Discover Boone County events calendar · official Zionsville website.

Who Zionsville Suits

Zionsville suits buyers who put schools, character and lot size first and have the budget. Buyers who need entry-level prices often look next door in Whitestown. Weighing it against the others? See Greenwood vs Franklin vs Zionsville.
