Moving Forward to a New Season of Life Is It Time?

Have you stayed in your home longer than you planned because the thought of moving feels overwhelming? You're not alone — and you don't have to manage it alone.

Senior Real Estate Downsizing Central Indiana

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated August 2026

Senior Real Estate August 2026 By Daniel Cope, Real Estate Broker — Your Realty Link ~6 min read

Have you stayed in your current home longer than you ever thought you would, simply because you feel overwhelmed at the thought of moving? Moving is not just picking up a few boxes and taking them to a new location — especially if you have lived in your home through recent life changes, may need extra help downsizing, or can no longer comfortably handle the maintenance of a single-family home and would consider a condo or a low-maintenance home with a few amenities.

We understand. It is a hard decision that creeps up on you, until one day you realize you need help. You have found it — we can help you manage the different stages of moving, and we have linked up with vendors who lend a helping hand to take the stress out of your next move. Learn more on our senior real estate page any time.

We Understand — It's a Hard Decision

For most people, the hardest part of a later-in-life move is not the house itself — it is everything the house holds, and the sheer number of decisions the move seems to require all at once. So it is completely normal to put it off. But the day you decide to move forward is the day it starts getting easier, because you no longer have to carry the whole thing by yourself.

Our job is to make the next chapter feel like a fresh start, not a mountain. We meet you where you are, go at your pace, and bring in the right help so nothing falls on your shoulders alone.

Let's Find Out Your Needs

Before we talk houses, we talk about you. These seven questions help us understand exactly what kind of help your move calls for — there are no wrong answers, and you do not need to have them all figured out yet:

  1. Do you have a current home you need to sell?
  2. Do you need to sell your current home before you move?
  3. Are you looking to downsize and own a home, or thinking about a retirement-community rental?
  4. Do you have family or close friends nearby to help — or will you need assistance clearing out furniture and household goods?
  5. Are you a single person or a couple, and over 55, 60, or 65?
  6. Do you think you may need a community with staged care — from independent living to assisted living?
  7. Would you like help with more than just the housing move — hiring a mover, hiring someone to organize the move, staging your current home, or setting up utilities in the new one?

Whatever your answers, they point us to the right plan and the right people. Not sure yet? That is exactly what a first, no-pressure conversation is for.

How We Help at Every Stage

Selling & timing. If you need to sell before you buy, we sequence the two sides so you are not carrying two homes or scrambling — using sale contingencies, rent-backs, and flexible closing dates to make it a single, smooth move.

Downsizing & clearing out. If family is not nearby to help, we connect you with senior move managers, estate-sale and donation services, and cleanout crews to handle the furniture and household goods you are ready to part with.

The whole move, not just the house. Beyond buying and selling, we can line up movers, professional organizers, home staging, and even help coordinate setting up utilities at your new place — one trusted point of contact instead of a dozen.

Own vs. rent vs. staged care. Whether you want to own a low-maintenance ranch or condo, rent in a 55+ community, or explore a community with independent-to-assisted-living options, we help you weigh the choices honestly and point you to the right resources for care-based options.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm overwhelmed and don't know where to start. What's the first step?

A single, no-pressure phone call. We start by simply learning your situation and what you want your next chapter to feel like — then we build a plan around it. You never have to have it all figured out before you reach out.

Do I have to sell my home before I can buy or move?

Not necessarily. Every situation is different. We help you sequence the sale and the next move using tools like sale contingencies, rent-backs, and negotiated closing dates so most clients move once, directly from the old home to the new one.

Can you help with the parts that aren't real estate — movers, organizing, utilities?

Yes. We have linked up with trusted local vendors — movers, senior move managers, organizers, stagers, and cleanout services — and can help coordinate the pieces, including setting up utilities, so the whole move is easier.

What if I need a community with assisted living, not just a home?

We'll help you weigh owning, renting, and staged-care options and point you to the right resources. Our expertise is the real estate side — selling your home and finding the right next place — and we're glad to connect you for care-based decisions.

Ready to Move Forward? Let's Start With Your Needs

Tell us a little about your situation — where you are, where you'd like to be, and what feels overwhelming. Our senior specialists will listen first and help you plan a move that fits your life, at your pace.
