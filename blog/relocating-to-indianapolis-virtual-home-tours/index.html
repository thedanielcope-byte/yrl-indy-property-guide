Relocating to Indianapolis? Tour Homes Before You Fly

How out-of-state buyers use private live-video showings to shortlist Central Indiana homes — and only travel for the finalists.

Buying a Home Relocation Central Indiana

✔ Reviewed by Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker · MIBOR member · Updated August 2026

Buying a Home August 2026 By Daniel Cope, Real Estate Broker — Your Realty Link ~7 min read

Moving to Indianapolis from out of state is exciting — and the housing piece can feel impossible to manage from a distance. You can not exactly fly in every weekend to tour homes. The good news: you do not have to. With private, live-video showings, you can tour Central Indiana homes from wherever you are now, shortlist the best, and save an in-person trip for the true finalists. Here is how relocation buyers use virtual tours to move with confidence. Start with our full relocation guide, then let us set up your first tour.

The Relocation Problem

When you are relocating for a job, the timeline is rarely generous. You may have a start date bearing down, a home to sell where you are now, and only a weekend or two to find a place in a metro you barely know. Trying to cram a dozen showings into one frantic trip leads to rushed decisions — or worse, flying out and finding nothing that works. Virtual tours change the whole rhythm of the process.

How Virtual Tours Fix It

Instead of touring on a deadline, you tour continuously from home. You tell us the areas and the must-haves, and as homes come up, one of our agents walks each one for you on a live video call. You direct the camera, ask about the neighborhood and the mechanicals, and get an honest read — the same as if you were standing there. Over a couple of weeks, you build a real shortlist without booking a single flight.

Then, when you do travel, your trip is efficient and low-stress: you are visiting three or four homes you already love to confirm the winner, not starting from scratch. Many relocation buyers even write competitive offers based on a great live-video tour when the timing demands it.

Getting to Know Central Indiana Remotely

Part of relocating is choosing where to land, not just which house. Central Indiana offers a lot of very different lifestyles within a short drive — top-rated suburbs like Carmel, Fishers, Westfield, and Zionsville; established, more affordable areas closer in; and charming small towns a bit further out. On a live-video tour we can show you the street and the surroundings too, and point you to the neighborhoods that fit your commute, budget, and priorities.

Be Ready to Move

Relocation markets reward preparation. Get pre-approved early so you can act the moment the right home appears, and lean on a local buyer's agent who knows the market and will represent your interests from afar. With the right team and virtual tours, buying from a distance can feel surprisingly calm — and even a little exciting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really buy a home in Indianapolis without seeing it in person first?

Many relocation buyers do. A thorough private live-video showing, combined with a trusted local agent and a home inspection, gives you a solid basis to buy with confidence. Whenever the timeline allows, we still recommend an in-person visit before closing — but virtual tours make a remote purchase genuinely workable.

How many homes can I tour virtually?

As many as you like. That is the advantage — there is no travel cost to touring one more home, so you can be selective and only spend your in-person trip on the finalists.

Which Indianapolis suburbs are best for families relocating?

It depends on your commute, budget, and priorities, but Hamilton County towns like Carmel, Fishers, Westfield, and Noblesville are perennial favorites for schools and amenities, while areas like Greenwood, Avon, and Brownsburg offer strong value. We will help you match the area to your needs.
