What Is a Virtual Open House? Touring Homes From Anywhere

You do not have to be in the room to really see a home. Here is how virtual open houses work — and how ours are different.

Buying a Home Live-Video Tours Central Indiana
Buying a Home August 2026 By Daniel Cope, Real Estate Broker — Your Realty Link ~7 min read

Not long ago, seeing a home meant getting in the car. Today, a growing share of Central Indiana buyers take their first tour without leaving the house — through a virtual open house. But virtual can mean several different things, and they are not all equally useful. This guide explains what a virtual open house actually is, the formats you will run into, and how Your Realty Link runs ours as private, live-video showings so you get a real tour with real answers, wherever you are.

What a Virtual Open House Is

A virtual open house is simply a way to tour a home remotely, over video, instead of walking through it in person. The goal is the same as a traditional open house — see the home, get your questions answered, and decide whether it is worth pursuing — but you attend from your phone, tablet, or laptop. That means you can tour a Fishers home from a hotel in Denver, or a Greenwood ranch from your office on a break.

The Different Types You Will See

Not all virtual tours are created equal. The most common formats are: a live social broadcast (an agent walks the home on a public stream you can watch and comment on), a pre-recorded walkthrough video (a produced tour you watch anytime), a self-guided 3D tour (a clickable dollhouse model you explore yourself), and a private live-video showing (a one-on-one live call just for you).

Recorded videos and 3D tours are great for a first glance, but they share one limit: you can not ask a question and get an answer in the moment, or say go back and show me that ceiling again. That is the gap a live, private showing fills.

How Your Realty Link Virtual Open Houses Work

Our virtual open houses are private, live-video showings. You pick a time, and one of our agents goes to the home and tours it for you on a live call over FaceTime, Zoom, or Google Meet. You are in control: tell the agent to open the closets, check the water pressure, look closely at the roofline out the window, or linger in the kitchen. You get honest, real-time answers — not a polished highlight reel.

It is a real showing in every way that matters, just with our feet on the ground and your eyes on the screen. And because it is private, the whole tour is built around your questions and your list — not a crowd of strangers.

Why Virtual Is So Powerful Right Now

Virtual showings quietly solve some of the hardest problems in home-buying. For families relocating to Indianapolis, they turn a stressful, expensive house-hunting trip into a calm process of touring remotely and flying in only for the finalists. For busy professionals, they make it possible to see homes the day they hit the market instead of waiting for the weekend. And for local buyers, they are a fast, no-pressure first pass before committing a Saturday.

In a market where the best homes can move quickly, being able to see a listing today — from wherever you are — is a genuine advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a virtual open house?

For a Your Realty Link live-video showing, you just tell us which home you want to see and pick a time. We send you a simple video call link — no app or account required beyond FaceTime, Zoom, or Google Meet. At the scheduled time, our agent tours the home live while you watch and ask questions.

Can I see any home virtually, or only certain ones?

We can arrange a private live-video showing of essentially any home on the market in Central Indiana, not just our own listings. If it is available to tour, we can show it to you on video.

Is a virtual tour enough to make an offer?

It can be — many buyers, especially those relocating, do exactly that. But whenever possible we recommend an in-person visit (or a trusted local set of eyes) before closing, so there are no surprises. A live-video showing is a strong basis for the decision, and we will always give you our honest read.
