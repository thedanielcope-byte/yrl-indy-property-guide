New Whiteland is a small suburban town in Pleasant Township that got its start in the 1950s when US-31 was rerouted through the area, incorporating in 1954. It sits immediately next to Whiteland and shares the Clark-Pleasant Community School Corporation, whose Whiteland Community High School (the Warriors) anchors the community. Housing runs from mid-century ranches near the older town core to newer subdivisions filling in along the fast-growing southern Indianapolis and Greenwood corridor. Your Realty Link serves buyers and sellers throughout New Whiteland and Johnson County, with experienced MIBOR agents who know the local market.

Living in New Whiteland, Indiana

New Whiteland offers the kind of Johnson County lifestyle that keeps buyers interested — quick US-31 and I-65 access to Indianapolis, generally more affordable than the Greenwood core, clark-Pleasant schools and Whiteland Community High School. Everyday shopping, dining, parks, and schools are close by, and the surrounding area adds even more. It's a community that suits families and professionals looking for New Whiteland-area value with metro access.

Commute from New Whiteland to Indianapolis

New Whiteland is about 25 to 30 minutes from downtown Indianapolis (roughly 20 miles south), with US-31 running directly through town and I-65 reached at the Whiteland interchange just east. Main routes serving the area include US-31, I-65, SR-135. For buyers who want Johnson County value with access to the wider Indianapolis metro, New Whiteland is a practical choice.
