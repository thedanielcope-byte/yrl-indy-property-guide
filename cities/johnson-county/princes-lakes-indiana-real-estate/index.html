Prince's Lakes is a small private-lake town in rural Nineveh Township at the southern edge of Johnson County, incorporated in 1956 and named for four namesake lakes within the community. Life here centers on the water, with boating, fishing, and swimming, and East Lake as the largest body of water. The town sits near Brown County and Camp Atterbury, drawing buyers who want an affordable lakefront or cottage lifestyle within the Indianapolis metro. Homes range from modest cottages to lakefront properties with docks and water views, so waterfront lots carry a premium. Living in Prince's Lakes, Indiana

Prince's Lakes offers the kind of Johnson County lifestyle that keeps buyers interested — private-lake town built around four namesake lakes with boating, fishing, and swimming, served by Indian Creek Schools (Nineveh-Hensley-Jackson), affordable cottage and lakefront homes in rural south Johnson County near Brown County. Everyday shopping, dining, parks, and schools are close by, and the surrounding area adds even more. It's a community that suits families and professionals looking for Prince's Lakes-area value with metro access.

Commute from Prince's Lakes to Indianapolis

Prince's Lakes is about 45 minutes (roughly 35 miles) from downtown Indianapolis via SR-135 north to Greenwood, then I-65. Main routes serving the area include SR-135, I-65 (via Franklin).
