Frankton is a small town in northern Madison County, about 12 miles northwest of Anderson, with SR-128 running straight through its center and Pipe Creek crossing the north end. Laid out in 1853 and incorporated in 1871, it has kept a tight-knit small-town feel and is home to Frankton Junior-Senior High School in the Frankton-Lapel district. Its signature event is the annual Heritage Days Festival, held every fall since 1975. Housing runs from older homes near the town center to newer construction on the edges. Commute from Frankton to Indianapolis

Frankton is a quiet northern Madison County town about 12 miles northwest of Anderson and roughly an hour from downtown Indianapolis via Anderson and I-69.
