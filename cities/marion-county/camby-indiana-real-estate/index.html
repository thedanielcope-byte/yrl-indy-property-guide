Camby is a growing southwest-side community centered along Camby Road near State Road 67 (Kentucky Avenue) in western Decatur Township, Marion County, with edges reaching into Hendricks and Morgan counties. A historic railroad and farming settlement once known as West Union Station, it has since filled in with newer subdivisions and new-construction neighborhoods. Buyers are drawn by relatively affordable family homes, new builds, and quick access to I-465, the Indianapolis airport, Plainfield, and Mooresville. Most students attend the Metropolitan School District of Decatur Township, feeding Decatur Central High School, while the southwest edge near Mooresville is served by Mooresville schools. Living in Camby, Indiana

Camby offers the kind of Marion County lifestyle that keeps buyers interested — southwest-side growth corridor along SR-67 / Kentucky Avenue in Decatur Township, new-construction subdivisions and affordable family homes, Decatur Central schools, quick access to I-465, the airport, Plainfield, and Mooresville. Everyday shopping, dining, parks, and schools are close by, and the surrounding area adds even more. It's a community that suits families and professionals looking for Camby-area value with metro access.

Schools in Camby, Indiana

Camby is served by Metropolitan School District of Decatur Township (Decatur Central); the far southwest / Morgan-County edge is served by Mooresville Consolidated School Corporation. School district boundaries are an important factor for many buyers, and assignment depends on the exact address — a Your Realty Link agent can help you identify the right neighborhoods based on your preferred school assignment.

Commute from Camby to Indianapolis

Camby is about 25 minutes (roughly 13 to 15 miles) from downtown Indianapolis via SR-67 (Kentucky Avenue) to I-465 and I-70. Main routes serving the area include SR-67 (Kentucky Avenue), I-465, I-70. For buyers who want Marion County value with access to the wider Indianapolis metro, Camby is a practical choice.
