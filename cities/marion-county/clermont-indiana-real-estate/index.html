Clermont is a small town on the west and northwest side of Indianapolis in Marion County's Pike and Wayne townships, bordering Brownsburg to the west. Since 1970 it has been an 'included town' under Unigov — legally part of Indianapolis while keeping its own town government. It is best known as the home of Lucas Oil Raceway, a drag strip, paved oval, and road course that hosts the NHRA U.S. Nationals over Labor Day weekend. Students are served primarily by MSD of Wayne Township and Ben Davis High School. It is a small, largely built-out community with older housing, so inventory is limited — buyers here are typically drawn by the west-side location, raceway proximity, and Brownsburg and Speedway access. Living in Clermont, Indiana

Clermont offers the kind of Marion County lifestyle that keeps buyers interested — home of Lucas Oil Raceway, host of the NHRA U.S. Nationals each Labor Day weekend, self-governing 'included town' on Indy's west side with quick US-136 / I-465 access downtown, zoned to MSD of Wayne Township (Ben Davis), just west of Speedway and next to Brownsburg. Everyday shopping, dining, parks, and schools are close by, and the surrounding area adds even more. It's a community that suits families and professionals looking for Clermont-area value with metro access.

Schools in Clermont, Indiana

Clermont is served by Metropolitan School District of Wayne Township (Ben Davis schools), with a small portion in MSD of Pike Township. School district boundaries are an important factor for many buyers, and assignment depends on the exact address — a Your Realty Link agent can help you identify the right neighborhoods based on your preferred school assignment.

Commute from Clermont to Indianapolis

Clermont is on the west side of Indianapolis, about 10 to 12 miles (roughly 15 to 20 minutes) from downtown via US-136 (Crawfordsville Road) and I-465 to I-65/I-70. Main routes serving the area include US-136, I-465, I-74. For buyers who want Marion County value with access to the wider Indianapolis metro, Clermont is a practical choice.
