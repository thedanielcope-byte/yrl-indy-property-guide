Brooklyn is a small Morgan County town in Clay and Brown townships, sitting between Mooresville and Martinsville just off State Road 67, with White Lick Creek running along its east side. It was laid out in 1854 as the railroad came through, and the surrounding area has documented Underground Railroad history. Just west of town, the Goethe Link Observatory is known for its spring daffodil gardens. Local children attend MSD of Martinsville schools, including Brooklyn Elementary STEM Academy in town, and the town has grown notably over the past decade. Living in Brooklyn, Indiana

Brooklyn offers the kind of Morgan County lifestyle that keeps buyers interested — goethe Link Observatory and its spring daffodil gardens just west of town, historic railroad town (platted 1854) zoned to MSD of Martinsville, between Mooresville and Martinsville off SR-67, about half an hour from downtown Indianapolis. Everyday shopping, dining, parks, and schools are close by, and the surrounding area adds even more. Schools in Brooklyn, Indiana

Brooklyn is served by Metropolitan School District of Martinsville (Brooklyn Elementary STEM Academy). School district boundaries are an important factor for many buyers, and assignment depends on the exact address — a Your Realty Link agent can help you identify the right neighborhoods based on your preferred school assignment.

Commute from Brooklyn to Indianapolis

Brooklyn is about 21 miles (roughly 30 to 35 minutes) from downtown Indianapolis via SR-67 north through Mooresville, with the SR-37/I-69 corridor also close by. Main routes serving the area include SR-67, SR-37/I-69, SR-42, SR-144. For buyers who want Morgan County value with access to the wider Indianapolis metro, Brooklyn is a practical choice.
