Bargersville and Whiteland are two Central Indiana communities buyers often weigh against each other. Both sit in Johnson County, so this really comes down to feel, price, and schools rather than geography. Here's how they actually compare — not the marketing, the real differences.

Home Prices

Bargersville homes typically run the $300s to the $600s+, while Whiteland generally lands in the $250s to the $400s+. Bargersville generally carries a premium, while Whiteland tends to give you more home per dollar. Compare current listings on our Bargersville and Whiteland pages, and remember that a local agent's read on value matters more than any published range.

Schools

Bargersville is served by Center Grove Schools; Whiteland is served by Clark-Pleasant Schools. Both are worth touring in person rather than judging from rankings, and the assigned schools depend on the exact address. See our Central Indiana school district guides to compare boundaries and confirm attendance for a specific home.

Lifestyle & Character

Bargersville is a Johnson County community known for its local character, Center Grove Schools, and convenient access to the greater Indianapolis area. Whiteland is a Johnson County community known for its local character, Clark-Pleasant Schools, and convenient access to the greater Indianapolis area. The two feel genuinely different day to day, and standing in each usually settles the choice faster than any list can.

Commute

Bargersville is about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis. Whiteland is about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis. For many buyers the deciding factor isn't the suburb at all — it's which corridor matches the drive you'll actually make every day.

The Honest Verdict

Both are strong choices, and most buyers should tour each before deciding. Since they're in the same county, you can see homes in both on the same afternoon. If value and more home per dollar matter most, Whiteland tends to win on price; if the prestige, amenities, feel of Bargersville is what you're after, that's where to lean. Frequently Asked Questions — Bargersville vs Whiteland

Is Bargersville or Whiteland more expensive?

Bargersville is generally the pricier of the two — it typically runs the $300s to the $600s+, versus the $250s to the $400s+ for Whiteland. At the same budget you'll usually get more home in Whiteland.

Which has better schools, Bargersville or Whiteland?

Bargersville is served by Center Grove Schools and Whiteland by Clark-Pleasant Schools. Both are worth touring in person, and the assigned schools depend on the specific address — a Your Realty Link agent can confirm attendance for any home.

Which is a shorter commute to downtown Indianapolis?

Bargersville is about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis. Whiteland is about 25 minutes to downtown Indianapolis. The better pick depends on which corridor matches your daily drive.

Is Bargersville or Whiteland better for my family?

It depends on your priorities. Bargersville is best for buyers who want newer homes and small-town character south of Indy, while Whiteland is best for buyers who want affordable new construction in Clark-Pleasant schools.
