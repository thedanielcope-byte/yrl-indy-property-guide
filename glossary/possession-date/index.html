What Is the Possession Date?

The day the keys actually change hands, which is not always the same day you close.

📘 Real Estate Term 📍 Indiana 🔑 Buyers & Sellers

Quick Answer

Most Central Indiana contracts set possession at closing, but buyer and seller can agree to a different date, such as a short post-closing occupancy for the seller. Whatever is agreed goes in writing in the purchase agreement.

Most common arrangement
Possession at closing
Alternative
Seller stays a few extra days post-closing
Where it's specified
The purchase agreement
Why it matters
Determines who is responsible for the home and utilities

Same-Day vs. Delayed Possession

When possession happens at closing, the buyer typically gets keys once the deed and funds have recorded, sometimes the same afternoon. When a seller needs extra time to move, the parties can negotiate a delayed or post-closing occupancy period, often with a daily fee to compensate the buyer.

Whichever arrangement you choose, put the exact date, time, and any occupancy fee in writing so there is no ambiguity.

Protecting Yourself Around the Possession Date

If the seller will occupy the home after closing, buyers should consider a short-term occupancy agreement outlining daily rent, a security deposit, and what happens if the seller does not vacate on time. A final walk-through right before closing helps confirm the home's condition before keys change hands.

Our agents help negotiate possession terms that work for both sides, especially in a competitive market where sellers may need a little extra time.

Frequently Asked Questions — Possession date

Does possession always happen on closing day?

Most often, yes, but it can be negotiated to happen a few days before or after closing.

What if the seller wants to stay after closing?

Buyer and seller can agree to a post-closing occupancy period, typically with a daily fee in writing.

Should I schedule the final walk-through around possession?

Yes, plan your final walk-through close to closing so the home's condition matches what was agreed.

What happens if the seller won't leave on the possession date?

This should be addressed in the occupancy agreement, which can include penalties for overstaying.
