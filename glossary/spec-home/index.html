What Is a Spec Home?

A new build already underway, with finishes largely decided and often ready to close sooner than a custom home.

📘 Real Estate Term 📍 Indiana 🔑 Buyers & Sellers

Quick Answer

A spec home is built by a developer or builder ahead of finding a buyer, with the floor plan and most finishes already chosen. Because fewer decisions remain, a spec home can often close faster than a fully custom-built home, which makes it a popular option for buyers on a tighter timeline.

Buyer involved during design
No, or only minimally
Finish selections
Usually already made by the builder
Typical advantage
Often a faster close than a custom build
Where to see one
Builder show homes or move-in-ready listings

How a Spec Home Differs From a Custom Build

With a custom build, the buyer works with the builder from the start on the floor plan, lot, and finishes. A spec home skips much of that process. The builder makes the design and finish decisions in advance, often based on what tends to sell well in that community, then lists the home for sale during or after construction.

This can be a good fit if you want a new home without months of selection meetings, though it also means less control over the final look.

What to Check Before You Buy a Spec Home

Ask where the home is in the construction timeline and whether a certificate of occupancy has been issued yet, since that affects your realistic move-in date. Also ask what is still adjustable, some builders allow late-stage buyers to swap minor finishes if construction has not reached that stage yet.

Review the builder warranty just as you would on any new-construction purchase, and have the home inspected even though it has never been lived in.

Frequently Asked Questions — Spec home

Is a spec home the same as a model home?

No. A model home is used to showcase a floor plan to shoppers and is not usually for sale. A spec home is built to be sold, sometimes to a buyer who never saw it under construction.

Can I still make changes to a spec home?

It depends on the construction stage. Early in the process, some builders allow limited finish changes. Once drywall or flooring is in, most selections are locked.

Are spec homes move-in ready?

Many are complete or nearly complete by the time they are listed, which is one of their main appeals for buyers who need to move quickly.

Do spec homes cost more or less than custom homes?

It varies by builder and community. Since the builder absorbs some of the selection process, pricing is generally comparable to a similarly finished custom home in the same neighborhood.
