Your Realty Link Launches Dedicated Senior Real Estate Resource for Central Indiana

The free guide helps homeowners over 55 downsize, right-size, and find one-level and 55-and-older living across the Indianapolis area.

INDIANAPOLIS, Ind., Sept. 18, 2026 — Your Realty Link, a full-service real estate brokerage based in Indianapolis, has launched a dedicated senior real estate resource for homeowners navigating a move after age 55. The free guide, available at yourrealtylink.com/senior-living, covers downsizing, right-sizing, one-level living, and 55-and-older communities across Central Indiana.

A move later in life is often more than a change of address. Many homeowners are leaving a house they have owned for decades, timing that sale against the search for a smaller or single-level home, and making the decision on a schedule set by health or family.

“Selling the home you raised a family in is a different kind of move, and it deserves a different kind of guidance. We built this resource so people over 55 and their families can weigh their options — downsizing, one-level living, 55-and-older communities — before they have to make a decision.”— Janet Giles-Schultz, Principal Broker and Owner of Your Realty Link

The resource explains how a senior real estate specialist can help, what to consider when financing a home later in life, and how to manage the logistics of the move itself. It also connects readers with the brokerage's senior specialists, Janet Giles-Schultz and Elizabeth Cottler, and with a search for one-level and 55-and-older homes in the Indianapolis market.

Your Realty Link serves buyers, sellers, and investors across Central Indiana from its office on Indianapolis's south side.

For more information, visit yourrealtylink.com/senior-living.
