Buy Foreclosures in Central Indiana

HUD sales, VA repossessions, and Fannie Mae foreclosures — and an experienced Your Realty Link agent to help you find the right one and close it.

Some of the best values in Central Indiana real estate are foreclosures and bank-owned homes — and Your Realty Link has agents who work with them. Whether you're an investor, a flipper, or a buyer hoping to land a diamond in the rough, we can help you search, evaluate, bid, and close on HUD sales, VA repossessions, and Fannie Mae foreclosures. Click any program below to start searching, then reach out and we'll pair you with an agent who knows how these purchases work.

HUD Homes

Foreclosed FHA-insured properties sold by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. HUD homes are bid through a registered selling broker — we'll walk you through it.

Search HUD Homes →

VA Repossessions

VA-owned (REO) homes from foreclosed VA loans, managed by VRM. Open to any buyer — not just veterans — often with flexible Vendee financing.

Search VA Homes →

Fannie Mae Foreclosures

Fannie Mae REO homes listed on HomePath, frequently with special financing and lower down-payment options for owner-occupants.

Search HomePath →
