## Central Indiana (all 17 counties)

September numbers are in, and the fall market showed up right on schedule. The median home sold for $320,000, still up about 1.3% from last September, so prices are holding. 2,549 homes sold, down about 3.5% from a year ago.

Here's what actually changed: there are 7,405 homes on the market now, up more than 16% from last year, and homes took a median of 27 days to sell instead of 20. Supply is up to 2.9 months.

Buyers, this is the most choice and the most negotiating room you've had in years, and fall sellers tend to be motivated. Sellers, you can still do well, but the homes that sell are the ones priced to today's market, not to last spring's. I break this down for all 17 counties every month. Want yours? Send me a message.
