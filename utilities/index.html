Central Indiana Utilities & Setup Guide

Just bought or about to move? Here is who to call to turn on electric, gas, water, sewer, trash, and internet across the Indianapolis metro — with a city-by-city directory below.

⚡ Electric & Gas 🚰 Water & Sewer 🗑️ Trash 🌐 Internet

Quick Answer

In most of Central Indiana, set up electricity with AES Indiana or Duke Energy, natural gas with Citizens Energy Group or CenterPoint Energy, and water/sewer with Citizens Water, Indiana American Water, or your town office. Trash is either on your city bill or a private hauler. Service areas follow your exact address — use the city-by-city guide below, and call Your Realty Link at 317-997-7404 if you need a hand.

One of the small stresses of moving is figuring out who provides what. In Central Indiana the answer depends on which county and even which side of town you are on — utility territories often follow property lines, not city limits. Here is the big picture, followed by a directory for all 62 communities we serve.

Who Provides What

⚡ Electricity

  • AES Indiana — 888-261-8222 · Website ↗ Indianapolis / Marion County and parts of the metro
  • Duke Energy Indiana — 800-521-2232 · Website ↗ much of the surrounding suburban and outlying area
  • Rural electric co-ops (REMC) NineStar, Hendricks Power, Boone REMC, Johnson County REMC, RushShelby Energy, SCI REMC and others serve rural addresses
  • Municipal electric some towns run their own — Anderson (AMLP), Greenfield (Power & Light), Pendleton, Edinburgh, Bargersville

🔥 Natural gas

  • Citizens Energy Group — 317-924-3311 · Website ↗ Indianapolis / Marion County and the inner ring
  • CenterPoint Energy — 800-227-1376 · Website ↗ most of the surrounding counties (formerly Vectren)
  • Propane some rural towns have no natural-gas main and use propane instead

🚰 Water & sewer

  • Citizens Energy Group (Citizens Water) — 317-924-3311 · Website ↗ Indianapolis and much of Marion County
  • Indiana American Water — 800-492-8373 · Website ↗ Greenwood, Noblesville, Franklin and many suburbs
  • Municipal water & sewer most towns run their own utility — set up water and sewer with the city or town office
  • Private well & septic common on rural and edge-of-town properties

🗑️ Trash & recycling

  • City / town collection many communities include trash on the municipal utility bill
  • Private subscription haulers elsewhere you choose a hauler — Republic Services, Ray's Trash, Best Way Disposal or GFL are common locally

🌐 Internet

  • Cable Xfinity (Comcast) and Spectrum cover most of the metro
  • Fiber AT&T Fiber, Metronet, Brightspeed, plus regional fiber (NineStar, Endeavor, JCFiber)
  • 5G home & satellite T-Mobile / Verizon 5G Home and Starlink fill in rural gaps
